## Stage 3: Dialectical Analysis

### Steelmanned Argument
HN's reaction is an early warning about trust: even if government-vetted access is temporary, technical users may update toward architectures they can run, audit, and keep. This is exactly the reputational cost that a state-mediated frontier model regime risks creating.

2. Frontier capability gaps may outweigh sovereignty concerns for many users.
