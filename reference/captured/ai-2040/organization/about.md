# AI Futures Project

The **AI Futures Project** is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit research organization (EIN 99-4320292). We are funded entirely by charitable donations and grants.

## Contact

You can reach us at <info@ai-futures.org> or `[firstname]@ai-futures.org`. We look forward to hearing from you.

## Team

![Portrait of Daniel Kokotajlo](https://ai-futures.org/_next/image?url=%2FDaniel.jpeg&w=256&q=75&dpl=dpl_8xSeDWrNEtb1LFLDqZg8Pf9sgTUf)

[**Daniel Kokotajlo**](https://x.com/DKokotajlo)Executive Director

Daniel oversees our research and policy recommendations. He previously worked as a governance researcher at OpenAI on scenario planning. When he left OpenAI, he [called for better transparency](https://www.nytimes.com/2024/06/04/technology/openai-culture-whistleblowers.html) of top AI companies. In 2021, he wrote [What 2026 Looks Like](https://www.alignmentforum.org/posts/6Xgy6CAf2jqHhynHL/what-2026-looks-like), an AI scenario forecast for 2022-2026 that [held up well](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/u9Kr97di29CkMvjaj/evaluating-what-2026-looks-like-so-far). See also his [Time100 AI 2024 profile](https://time.com/7012881/daniel-kokotajlo/).

![Portrait of Eli Lifland](https://ai-futures.org/_next/image?url=%2FEli.jpeg&w=256&q=75&dpl=dpl_8xSeDWrNEtb1LFLDqZg8Pf9sgTUf)

[**Eli Lifland**](https://x.com/eli_lifland)Research Lead

Eli works on scenario forecasting and specializes in forecasting AI capabilities. He also co-founded and advises [Sage](https://sage-future.org/), which builds [interactive AI explainers](https://theaidigest.org/). He previously worked on [Elicit](https://elicit.com/), an AI-powered research assistant, and co-created [TextAttack](https://github.com/QData/TextAttack), a Python framework for adversarial examples in text. He ranks first on the [RAND Forecasting Initiative](https://www.rand.org/global-and-emerging-risks/forecasting-initiative.html) all-time leaderboard.

![Portrait of Thomas Larsen](https://ai-futures.org/_next/image?url=%2FThomas.jpeg&w=256&q=75&dpl=dpl_8xSeDWrNEtb1LFLDqZg8Pf9sgTUf)

[**Thomas Larsen**](https://www.linkedin.com/in/thomas-larsen/)Research Lead

Thomas works on scenario forecasting and focuses on understanding the goals and real-world impacts of AI agents. He previously founded the [Center for AI Policy](https://www.aipolicy.us/), an AI safety advocacy organization, and worked on AI safety research at the [Machine Intelligence Research Institute](https://intelligence.org/).

![Portrait of Romeo Dean](https://ai-futures.org/_next/image?url=%2FRomeo.png&w=256&q=75&dpl=dpl_8xSeDWrNEtb1LFLDqZg8Pf9sgTUf)

[**Romeo Dean**](https://www.linkedin.com/in/romeo-dean-789313200/)Researcher

Romeo specializes in forecasting AI chip production and usage. He graduated cum laude from Harvard University with a concurrent master's in computer science and a research focus in security and hardware. He previously was an AI Policy Fellow at the Institute for AI Policy and Strategy.

![Portrait of Lauren Mangla](https://ai-futures.org/_next/image?url=%2FLauren.jpeg&w=256&q=75&dpl=dpl_8xSeDWrNEtb1LFLDqZg8Pf9sgTUf)

[**Lauren Mangla**](https://www.linkedin.com/in/lauren-m-b91b2b1a3/)Chief Operating Officer

Lauren works on comms, media relations, hiring, and tabletop exercises, and manages the ops team. Previously at Constellation—an AI safety research center—she made strategic decisions on membership and events. Before that, she served as Executive Director of the Supervised Program for Alignment Research (SPAR), an AI safety fellowship. She's also held internships at NASA and in NYC policy and government.

![Portrait of Miles Kodama](https://ai-futures.org/_next/image?url=%2FMiles.jpg&w=256&q=75&dpl=dpl_8xSeDWrNEtb1LFLDqZg8Pf9sgTUf)

[**Miles Kodama**](https://mkodama.org/)Policy Researcher

Miles works on policy options to steer the development and deployment of transformative AI. He graduated from Williams College with a BA in physics. He was previously a summer research fellow at the [Centre for the Governance of AI](http://governance.ai/) and a contractor on the policy team at [METR](https://metr.org/).

![Portrait of Nicole Sanna](https://ai-futures.org/_next/image?url=%2FNicole.jpg&w=256&q=75&dpl=dpl_8xSeDWrNEtb1LFLDqZg8Pf9sgTUf)

[**Nicole Sanna**](https://www.linkedin.com/in/nicole-sanna/)Project Manager

Nicole Sanna handles operations and supports communications, media relations, and a range of other projects. Previously, she built and managed operations systems at the Atlas Fellowship, EA Funds, and Sentinel Bio. She graduated from Carnegie Mellon University with a Master's in Public Policy and Management.

![Portrait of Brendan Halstead](https://ai-futures.org/_next/image?url=%2FBrendan.png&w=256&q=75&dpl=dpl_8xSeDWrNEtb1LFLDqZg8Pf9sgTUf)

**Brendan Halstead**Research Contractor

Brendan works on forecasting AI capabilities and their strategic impacts. He was previously a [MATS](https://www.matsprogram.org/) scholar and an AI Policy Fellow at the [Institute for AI Policy and Strategy](https://www.iaps.ai/). He is completing a mathematics degree at the Massachusetts Institute of Technology.
