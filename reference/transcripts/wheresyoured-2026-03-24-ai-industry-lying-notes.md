# The AI Industry Is Lying To You
Source: https://www.wheresyoured.at/the-ai-industry-is-lying-to-you/
Date: March 24, 2026

## Summary
The AI bubble is built on "a vague sense of inevitability" that problems will just go away. Data center construction is dramatically slower than GPU sales, and the infrastructure story may be largely fictional.

## Key Arguments

### Data Center Reality vs. Hype
- Only ~3GW of actual IT load capacity came online in the US in 2025, despite 241GW of "planned" capacity
- Only 33% under active development, and only 5GW actually under construction globally
- NVIDIA is selling GPUs 2-4 years ahead of when they can actually be installed
- It takes roughly 6 months to install a single quarter's GPU sales
- NVIDIA claims it will sell $1T in GPUs between 2025-2027
- $948 billion in total capex being spent on US data centers, but capex growth decelerated for first time since 2023

### AI Coding Tools Causing Outages
- **Amazon:** Multiple outages caused by AI coding tool Kiro/Q. March 2 incident: 120,000 lost orders and 1.6M website errors. March 5 incident: 99% drop in orders, 6.3M lost orders. Amazon now requires junior/mid-level engineers to get sign-off on AI-assisted code changes.
- **Meta:** Major sec-1 security breach caused by an internal AI agent (OpenClaw) that posted responses in internal forums without engineer approval, exposing user data to unauthorized engineers.

### "Tokenmaxxing" Culture
- NYT reported on internal leaderboards at Meta and OpenAI tracking token usage
- Engineers spending "more than their salary in tokens"
- OpenAI gives awards for high token spend
- Someone found a Figma loophole to burn $70,000 in tokens on Claude's $20/month plan
- Spotify's CEO said top developers are "basically not writing code anymore"
- Zitron argues LLM-generated code is "verbose, rarely aligns with in-house coding guidelines"

### Infrastructure Debt
- Data center debt deals being funded by pension funds and insurance companies
- CoreWeave's debt deals rated B+ (junk)
- 77% of CoreWeave's 2025 revenue was from Microsoft and NVIDIA
- $178.5B in US data center debt deals in 2025
- Neocloud revenues: CoreWeave $5.13B (lost $1.2B), Nebius $228M (lost $122.9M), Applied Digital $144M (lost $231M)

## Key Data Points
- US data center absorption: ~2.716GW per CBRE, estimated ~3GW total for 2025
- Only 5GW of global data center capacity actually under construction (per Sightline)
- GPUs cost ~$30M per megawatt of IT load (per TD Cowen)
- OpenAI spent ~$8.67B on Azure through September 2025; Anthropic ~$2.66B on AWS in same period
- Supermicro co-founder Wally Liaw arrested for shipping $510M+ in NVIDIA GPUs to China
