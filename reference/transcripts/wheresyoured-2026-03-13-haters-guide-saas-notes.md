# Premium: The Hater's Guide To The SaaSpocalypse
Source: https://www.wheresyoured.at/hatersguide-saas/
Date: March 13, 2026

## Summary
The "SaaSpocalypse" narrative — that AI is destroying SaaS companies — is a cover story for a much larger problem: the end of the hyper-growth era in software, which Zitron calls the "Rot-Com Bubble."

## Key Arguments
- SaaS growth has been declining for years (declining growth and Net Revenue Retention per Bain data). The industry was built on the assumption of perpetual growth, which turned out to be false.
- AI is NOT replacing software. The claim that "anyone can write code now" misunderstands what building software actually involves (infrastructure, reliability, scaling, security, etc.).
- Private equity piled into SaaS between 2018-2022 (30-40% of PE deals were software), using debt based on recurring revenue and the assumption growth would never stop. Now they are stuck with overvalued "zombie SaaS companies."
- Venture capital vintages after 2018 are returning 0.8x to 1.2x TVPI.

## Enterprise AI Revenue Roundup
- **IBM:** $12.5bn "generative AI book of business since 2023" — 80% from consultancy (reselling others' models). Then stopped reporting this metric. Total revenue barely changed ($60.5bn in 2022 to $67.5bn in 2025).
- **Adobe:** "AI-first" revenue (Firefly etc.) ~$375M ARR, or ~1.5% of quarterly revenue. "AI-influenced" revenue surpassing $5bn ARR but definition is extremely broad.
- **Salesforce:** Agentforce ARR was $800M ($66M/month) for a company making $11.2bn/year. The reported $2.9bn figure included $1.1bn from the Informatica Cloud acquisition (not an AI product).
- **ServiceNow:** Claimed $600M in ACV from AI but net income has been flat since 2023.
- **Workday:** $400M ARR from AI products ($33M/month) on $2.5bn quarterly revenue.
- **Intuit:** Bragged about $90M in "AI efficiencies" while paying $100M+ for ChatGPT model access.
- **Microsoft, Google, Amazon, Box, Shopify, Atlassian:** Do not break out AI revenue.
- **Anthropic:** $5bn in lifetime revenue through March 2026 on $60bn of funding.
- **OpenAI:** Zitron believes it made "far less than $13 billion" based on his reporting.
- **Cursor:** $2bn annualized revenue ($166M/month) but raised $3bn in 2025 alone.
- **Harvey:** $190M ARR on $11bn valuation, after raising $500M+ in 2025.
- **Lovable:** $400M annualized revenue after raising $545M total.

## Data Points
- ~33% of venture funding went to SaaS in Q3 2025 (per Carta)
- SaaS PE acquisitions hit $250bn in 2021
- 20% of loans by public BDCs go to software firms
- 14 largest BDCs loaned $18bn across 1000 companies in 2024
