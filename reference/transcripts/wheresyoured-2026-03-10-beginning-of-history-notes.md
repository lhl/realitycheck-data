# The Beginning of History
Source: https://www.wheresyoured.at/the-beginning-of-history/
Date: March 10, 2026

## Summary
The Iran-US conflict and closure of the Strait of Hormuz will spike energy costs, inflation, and interest rates, devastating the debt-funded AI bubble. Anthropic's actual revenue figures don't add up.

## Key Arguments

### Geopolitical Shock to AI Bubble
- Iran conflict closed Strait of Hormuz (through which ~20% of global oil and LNG flows)
- Higher interest rates would make hundreds of billions in AI borrowing significantly more expensive
- Private credit, PE, and VC all depend on near-perpetual availability of cheap debt
- The AI bubble is uniquely fragile because it is built almost entirely on debt

### Anthropic Revenue Doesn't Add Up
- Anthropic CFO Krishna Rao's legal affidavit (March 9, 2026): "exceeding $5 billion" in lifetime revenue
- "Over $10 billion" in inference and training costs
- Annualized revenue timeline from leaks:
  - $2B (end of March 2025)
  - $4B (July 2025)
  - $7B (October 2025)
  - $9B+ (December 2025)
  - $13B (early Feb 2026)
  - $14B (Feb 12, 2026)
  - $19B (March 4, 2026 — "$6 billion added in February" per Dario Amodei at Morgan Stanley)
- Zitron's calculation: adding up all reported annualized revenues = ~$6.66B total, which exceeds the $5B lifetime figure
- Conclusion: Anthropic is measuring "annualized revenue" in some non-standard way

### Macro/Debt Data
- Oil spiked 30% overnight to over $100/barrel
- IMF: 10% energy price increase persisting for a year pushes global inflation up 40 basis points
- SoftBank trying to raise $40B loan while carrying $41.5B in existing debt maturing within 9 months
- PE fundraising: average 23 months (up from 16 in 2021)
- Private credit corporate borrower default rates hit 9.2% in 2025
- 14% of large banks' total loan commitments go to PE/private credit
